Privacy Policy - Pulse Music Player EQ

Last updated: November 2026

Pulse Music Player EQ ("we", "our", or "the app") is designed to provide high-definition offline audio playback and customized equalizer effects. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

1. 100% Offline & No Data Collection

Pulse Music Player EQ does not collect, record, transmit, or share any personal data, audio files, playlists, or usage statistics. The application runs strictly locally on your device with no remote servers.

2. Device Permissions Used

  • Read Audio / Media Permission (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used exclusively to scan, organize, and play your local audio tracks, music folders, and playlists.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): Used to keep music playing in the background with lock-screen playback controls and notification panel.
  • Modify Audio Settings (MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS): Required to apply hardware/DSP equalizer, dynamic bass boost, and compressor audio effects.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or transfer your personal data or local audio library to any third parties.

4. Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any information from children under the age of 13 or any other age group.
